About Krona

Less to remember.

Krona is being built to make everyday reminders and recurring obligations easier to capture, organise, and follow up on.

Our idea

Useful reminders should be easy to create and easy to find. Krona aims to help you keep track of the things that matter, from everyday tasks to recurring due dates.
