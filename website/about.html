About Cuenuvo

Less to remember.
More room to focus.

Cuenuvo is being built to make everyday reminders and recurring reminders easier to capture, organise, and follow up on.

Our idea

Useful reminders should be easy to create and easy to find. Cuenuvo aims to help you keep track of the things that matter, from everyday tasks to recurring due dates.

Built step by step

Cuenuvo is an evolving product. We’re working toward a clear, dependable experience and will describe features here as they become available.
